Who stands behind the content

We don't invent safety rules: we gather what has already worked in Ukraine and have it checked by people who know it from practice.

Veterans

The founder of Orizo Labs, a veteran himself, personally reviews and analyses the lessons on threats, shelters and behaviour in a crisis together with other veterans on our expert team. That way the advice works in real life, not just on paper.

Hands-on instructors

First aid, navigation and field skills are reviewed by instructors who teach people in person.

Official guidance

We rely on public civil-protection guidance and update lessons as threats change.

Who it's for

Families

Calmly prepare your home, children and loved ones — without panic and without overspending.

Schools and teachers

Ready-made lessons and exams for classes, explained in plain language.

Communities and volunteers

Joint preparation for residents, group training and an instructor log.

Europe

Ukraine's experience for people in Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia, Poland, Slovakia and other countries where preparedness has become part of life.

Civilian and protective only: how to recognise a threat and protect yourself and others. No combat tactics, weapons or instructions that could cause harm.
