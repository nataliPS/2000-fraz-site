A pocket instructor for service members and those preparing to serve: it teaches, guides training, analyses results and suggests the next step. The STS method and Ukrainian experience — even offline.

Why it's needed

An instructor isn't always there

Learning and training have to be constant, but there are not enough experienced instructors for everyone. The app guides you through the programme every day.

Results don't get lost

Time, hits, heart rate and load are recorded in the app, not in a notebook. You see your progress and what to fix.

Data security is a matter of life

Call signs instead of names, all records only on the phone. Locations and units are never shared.

What makes it special

Digital instructor

Spots a mistake in your results and suggests a specific exercise.

Combat heart rate

How results change under load: data from Garmin or Apple Watch.

Unit readiness

One screen for the commander: who is ready and who is falling behind.

Verified results

An exam with an instructor and a certificate; later, accreditation under ISO/IEC 17024.

Battle Fit 30

Our own 30-day fitness programme with tests at the start and the finish.

Hands-free

Pro
