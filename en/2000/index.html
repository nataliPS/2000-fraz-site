Questions

Do I need to sign up?

No. No account is needed; your progress is stored on your iPhone.

How do I move my progress to another phone?

Settings → “Backup” → “Save”: you can keep the file in Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, choose “Restore”.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → 2000 Phrases. Access lasts until the end of the paid period.

Will there be an Android version?

Yes, we are working on it. For now the app is available for iPhone.

Found a mistake in a phrase?

Write to us at support@orizolabs.com or use “Write to the developer” at the bottom of the home screen — the email will already include the app version and language.
