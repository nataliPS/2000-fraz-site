For investors

Orizo Labs builds learning apps on Ukraine's experience for two large audiences: Ukrainians living abroad, and Europeans for whom civil preparedness has become part of life.

Why now

The world has become unstable. The Baltic states, Poland and other countries live next to a real threat on their borders and are already preparing their citizens for a crisis: schools, communities, every family. Ukraine went down this road first, and its experience is needed right now.

4.4 M

Ukrainians hold temporary protection in the EU — a record as of August 2026. They need the language every day: at the doctor's, at work, with paperwork.

72 hours

of emergency supplies is what the European Commission advises every household to keep. Preparedness is becoming public policy across Europe.

since 2022

Ukraine has lived with air-raid alerts, blackouts and evacuations. We turn this hands-on experience into courses for everyone.

Safety and peace of mind are the greatest values of our time. We give people the knowledge that keeps them safe.
