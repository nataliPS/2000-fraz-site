About

Born from Ukrainian experience

Since 2022, millions of Ukrainians have found themselves in a new country within weeks, without the language, while air-raid alerts, blackouts and shelters became part of everyday life. We saw how much simple knowledge matters: what to tell a doctor, how to stop bleeding, what to pack in a go-bag.

Orizo Labs turns this experience into learning apps — for Ukrainians and for everyone who wants to be prepared. Our goal: everyone should have the knowledge that makes a new reality less frightening.

The experience we build on

Ukrainian resilience

Alerts, blackouts, evacuation, life after moving abroad — we teach what millions of Ukrainians have already lived through.

Veterans on the team

Orizo Labs' founders and experts include veterans with many years of experience, some of it at international level. We review preparedness courses as a team: veterans and hands-on instructors analyse the content together, so the advice works in real life.

Entrepreneurial experience

Our founder has built and grown his own business in Ukraine, including in wartime. That is why we spend resources carefully, test ideas fast and get products into people's hands.

Our values

Experience
