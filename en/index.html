Where we are going

We move from the most urgent needs of a person in a new situation towards deeper preparation and work.

  1. Now

    Languages for life

  2. Now

    Readiness and safety

    Readiness Academy: eight areas of knowledge, courses, scenarios, crisis mode.

  3. 2027

    Languages for work

    Job-specific courses: construction, care and healthcare, logistics, hospitality, IT.

  4. 2028

    Settling in

    Step-by-step guides to documents, banking, doctors, renting and schools in a specific country.
